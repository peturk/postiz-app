## Three art directions

All three are **photo-only bases**. Logo and lettering are **post-composite only**.

### Direction A — “Dispatch hour” (operational documentary)

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Scene** | One field technician (30–50s, Nordic features OK but not costume) at open service van or workshop threshold at start of shift. Tablet or rugged phone in hand showing a clean abstract UI (no readable fake brand). Soft rain or damp air; reflective asphalt; no tourist props. |
| **Framing** | Horizontal 2.6:1 master. Subject **right-of-center or center-right**; face/shoulders readable on desktop **and** in mobile center crop. Left third quieter (for profile-pic overlap). Eye level or slight three-quarter, 35–50mm equivalent, shallow–medium DOF (f/2.8–f/4). |
| **Light** | Overcast Icelandic morning (high, soft, cool 5500–6500K). Optional single warm practical (van interior LED or workshop bulb) on hands/device only. No golden-hour drama, no neon cyberpunk. |
| **Palette** | Basalt gray, wet black, muted moss, steel, navy workwear; warm skin + leather glove as only warm notes. Desaturated ~15–25% vs tourist stock. |
| **Negative space** | Upper-center to right-center clear sky or wall plane for 2–4 word headline composite. No busy signage. |
| **Typography pairing (composite)** | **Headline:** Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold or Inter Bold, white or near-white with soft dark scrim if needed. **Secondary (optional):** Inter Regular, tracking +2–4%. Max ~6 words total. |
| **Why it works** | Aligns with SaaS Hero authentic-human trend and FSM mobile-tech reality (Viewpoint). Premium without adventure-brochure energy. |
| **Anti-cliché Icelandic cue** | Weather + workwear + municipal/industrial edge, not landscape postcard. |

### Direction B — “Service landscape” (infra, not tourism)

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Scene** | Wide exterior of **working infrastructure** under Icelandic weather: roadside utility cabinet + service van, harbor industrial edge, geothermal plant periphery, or fiber/road crew at safe distance. Tiny human scale optional (figure ≤15% of frame). **No** waterfalls, glaciers, aurora, turf houses, or “iconic” tourist compositions. |
| **Framing** | Strong horizon in lower third; sky occupies ≥50% of height as clean field for type. Van or structure as mid-ground anchor slightly off-center. 24–35mm, deep DOF, documentary still (think editorial annual report, not travel magazine). |
| **Light** | Flat overcast or post-rain silver light; long soft shadows if any. Avoid HDR sky replacements and hyper-saturated moss. |
| **Palette** | Cool gray-green landscape, oxidized metal, yellow/orange safety accent **used sparingly** (one cone, one vest, one van stripe). |
| **Negative space** | Large sky / fog field across top half and center - primary type zone. Keep safety color out of type zone. |
| **Typography pairing (composite)** | **Headline:** IBM Plex Sans Medium or Söhne-class geometric, condensed slightly if needed for long Icelandic product name. Color: off-white or charcoal depending on sky value. Optional thin horizontal rule in brand accent. |
| **Why it works** | Encodes “operations at national scale” and Local Flavor (Adobe) without tourism clichés; DesignMarch-adjacent seriousness (design as infrastructure). |
| **Risk** | Can feel cold/empty if no human scale - mitigate with one small figure or open van door as life cue. |

### Direction C — “Hands, tools, truth” (tactile close-work)

| Spec | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Scene** | Tight–medium: gloved hands, tool, tablet edge, and a textured surface (wet metal, cable, concrete, moss-flecked equipment housing). Face optional / partial only. Emphasis on **material truth** (Adobe All the Feels). |
| **Framing** | Subject mass on **left-center** so profile pic does not fully bury the story; **right 40%** nearly empty for brand lockup. Macro–short tele (50–85mm), shallow DOF, tactile micro-contrast. |
| **Light** | Cool ambient + warm practical rim on metal edges (workshop tungsten ~3200K). Controlled speculars, not CGI chrome. |
| **Palette** | Near-monochrome graphite + one brand-consistent accent if known; otherwise oxidized copper / safety amber used at ≤5% of frame. |
| **Negative space** | Deliberate empty field right/upper-right for stacked lockup composite (`sporbok-lockup-stacked.png` or icon + typeset wordmark). |
| **Typography pairing (composite)** | Minimal: wordmark only **or** single short line in Inter Medium under lockup. Prefer **no slogan** on cover; let tactility sell premium. |
| **Why it works** | Maximum differentiation from tourism; highest “premium product photography” read; safest crop behavior on mobile if empty field is protected. |
| **Risk** | Can feel generic industrial if not grounded with Icelandic weather cues (condensation, outdoor cold light spill). |

## Typography recommendations

### Principles (evidence-backed)

- **[FACT]** 2026 B2B SaaS social type favors high-character geometric/humanist sans with personality beyond Helvetica clones: Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk, Manrope, IBM Plex, Söhne-class (UseVisuals 2026-08-03; Orbix 2026-08-06).
- **[FACT]** Bold type + microcopy is a named 2026 ad trend (SaaS Hero #7); kinetic/experimental type is for ads, **not** for Facebook covers that crop aggressively.
- **[FACT]** Snappa: covers with **too much text** or text-only backgrounds underperform as business covers; image should mostly speak alone.

### Recommended system for Sporbók cover (composite layer only)

| Role | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary display** | **Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold / Bold** or **Inter Bold** | Current B2B SaaS social standard; tall x-height survives mobile crop (UseVisuals). |
| **Secondary / UI-adjacent** | **IBM Plex Sans** or **Inter Regular** | Technical ops credibility without coder-bro monospaced cliché on a banner. |
| **Wordmark** | **Official `sporbok-wordmark.png` or typeset from brand master - never AI-generated glyphs** | Brand README + prior corruption failure. |
| **Mark** | **`sporbok-icon.png`** (dark tile) on light photo areas; if photo is dark, invert via proper brand inverse when available - do not regenerate. | Official assets only. |
| **Size / hierarchy** | Cover headline ≤ **2 lines**, ≤ **28–36 px** at 820-wide (scale up for 2× masters); wordmark height roughly **28–40 px** at display size. | Legibility under Facebook compression. |
| **Color** | White/off-white on dark scrim **or** charcoal on bright sky; 4.5:1 contrast minimum. Avoid rainbow gradients and glassmorphism type. | Premium B2B + accessibility. |
| **Avoid** | Script, blackletter “Viking,” oversized outlined AI fonts, long Icelandic paragraphs, fake UI text inside the generated photo. | Cliché + defect risk. |

### Compositing pipeline (mandatory)

1. Generate / shoot **photograph only** (no logos, no slogans, no fake app chrome with legible brand names).  
2. Place official PNGs + real fonts in Figma/Photoshop.  
3. Export sRGB JPG for FB (or PNG if logo edges need alpha then flatten).  
4. QA desktop **and** mobile crops; check profile-pic occlusion.
