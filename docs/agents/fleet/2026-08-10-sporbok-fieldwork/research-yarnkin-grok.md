## Observed facts

Labeled **FACT** (from cited sources) vs **INFERENCE** (synthesis for Yarnkin).

### Children’s publishing / tactile premium direction

1. **FACT** — Textured mixed media (collage + paint + pencil + digital texture) is currently the most popular professional children’s-book style because it signals handmade authenticity in a market aware of AI art; positioned for literary / gift / art-forward books ages 3–8.  
   Source: US Illustrations, 2026-01-07.

2. **FACT** — 2026 kids’ illustration trends list **textured mixed media** (watercolor washes, pencil, collage, paper grain, brush strokes, scanned handmade materials) as a major trend; publishers associate visible texture with craftsmanship vs AI-smooth images. Published 2026-06-30.  
   Source: Prayan Animation.

3. **FACT** — Industry commentary for 2026 emphasizes hand-drawn / imperfect / analogue work as a response to sameness of digital/AI imagery; nostalgia, comfort, hope; **seasonality framed as season-of-nature hooks** (“nuts for autumn”) rather than single holiday events.  
   Source: Bright Agency / Vicki Willden-Lebrecht, last updated 2026-01-07.

4. **FACT** — Agents and illustrators for 2026 repeatedly cite hand-printed, hand-painted, craft, and “human-touch” commissions as brands seek connection; positivity and reassurance are wanted.  
   Source: Creative Boom, 2025-12-18 (still cited as 2026 outlook; page active 2026-08).

5. **FACT** — Soft watercolor / gouache-adjacent styles remain best for gentle, bedtime, nature, literary picture books (warmth, safety, quiet beauty).  
   Source: US Illustrations.

6. **INFERENCE** — Yarnkin’s accepted “storybook + bedtime warmth” preference maps cleanly onto soft-gouache + layered tactile collage, **not** bold neon comic palettes or flat vector minimalism.

### Seasonal brand / Facebook cover practice

7. **FACT** — Strong Facebook covers communicate **one primary message in under two seconds**; seasonal campaign is a named use case; best practice is to treat the cover as a campaign module and **seasonal reset** (e.g. quarterly swap). Published 2026-04-07.  
   Source: InfluencerDB.

8. **FACT** — Cover design must be mobile-first; critical text/logos in center-safe area; leave profile-photo overlap clear.  
   Same source.

9. **FACT** — Display sizes commonly cited for 2026: **desktop 820×312**, **mobile 640×360**; recommended upload **851×315** sRGB; safe practice is keep key content in the central band; bottom-left profile overlap. Guide updated **2026-08-07** (inside window).  
   Source: ContentStudio.

10. **FACT** — Meta Help Center: cover loads fastest as sRGB JPG **851×315** under 100 KB; min 400×150; mobile overlap of profile picture ~40 px.  
    Source: Facebook Help Center.

### Reykjavík / Hallgrímskirkja / seasons

11. **FACT** — Hallgrímskirkja (Guðjón Samúelsson, design 1937) exterior columns inspired by Icelandic basalt, glaciers, waterfalls; white associated with snow and rushing water - landscape architecture, not generic Gothic.  
    Source: Your Friend in Reykjavík guide (content describes architecture; 403 on full fetch of one URL; corroborating eclipse-tour note 2026 cites basalt-column inspiration).

12. **FACT** — Reykjavík winter temps ~−5 to 2 °C; summer ~12–15 °C; daylight extremes (~4–5 h Dec vs ~21 h late June); spring thaw brings emerging color and coastal bloom; autumn short with moss/lava gold-red-brown; winter snow appears and melts (not permanent white blanket in the city). Guide last updated **2026-06-04**.  
    Source: Guide to Iceland.

13. **FACT** — Contemporary travel photography of Hallgrímskirkja (e.g. July 2026 social posts) still defaults to hero tower shots - the cliché Yarnkin must **avoid**.  
    Source: Instagram landscape posts dated ~2026-07-16.

### Mascot consistency (generic brand practice)

14. **FACT** — Effective mascots must stay memorable, recognizable, original, **consistent character**, flexible, multi-size, stylistically harmonic; used across logos, hero banners, merch without rewriting the character each time. Page marked 2026-08-04 in site metadata (inside window for retrieval).  
    Source: Design4Users / Tubik.

15. **INFERENCE** — For Yarnkin, “flexible” applies to **scene and lighting around** the mascot, **not** redrawing the yarnball or wordmark. Official PNGs are locked composite layers.

## Seasonal family system

One recognizable **Yarnkin composition grammar** across all four covers. Only season, light, and secondary palette shift.

### Fixed composition grammar (all seasons)

| Zone | Role | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Left–center midground** | Soft Reykjavík terrace / street edge + muted colorful corrugated houses (2–4 façades max) | Always present; never photoreal postcard |
| **Far center / center-right skyline** | Hallgrímskirkja as **side-lit basalt-column silhouette or ¾ profile**, partial height, often half-obscured by fog, yarn haze, or rooftops | Never full frontal hero postcard; never tourism blue-sky stock |
| **Horizon band** | Faxaflói / Esja suggestion as soft layered shapes, not labeled landmarks | Distant, simplified, gouache masses |
| **Foreground shelf** | Tactile yarn/plush “stage”: wool fibers, felt ground, soft collaged paper texture | Continuous across seasons - Yarnkin’s home ground |
| **Mascot plate (post-composite)** | Official yarnball PNG | Fixed safe-zone anchor (see Mascot + Crop) |
| **Wordmark plate (post-composite)** | Official plush wordmark PNG | Fixed safe-zone anchor; never re-lettered |
| **Typography in art** | None in AI/background layer | Text-free backgrounds only |

### Shared palette logic

| Role | Constant across year | Seasonal shift only |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yarnkin brand warmth** | Soft cream, milk-wool white, gentle peach-blush accents (bedtime warmth) | Intensity only |
| **Plush yarn ball hues** | Match official asset (do not recolor mascot) | Environment complements, does not recolor |
| **Architecture** | Weathered soft primaries (dusty red, ochre, teal, slate) on houses - always **muted** | Green/lichen/snow overlays by season |
| **Atmosphere** | Soft edge, low micro-contrast, paper grain, gouache scumble | Hue of sky + ground |
| **Avoid all year** | Neon, pure black, harsh HDR, photoreal glass reflections, stock-travel saturation | - |

### Shared art medium

- Soft gouache + dry-brush  
- Collaged felt/paper edges on foreground  
- Visible fiber and grain (anti-AI-smooth)  
- Storybook depth: 3 planes (fore / mid / far), gentle vignette  

**INFERENCE:** This is the storybook path the user preferred, with bedtime warmth in cream/blush and twilight’s city air without holiday coding.

### Shared “not tourism poster” rules for Hallgrímskirkja

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Basalt-column rhythm as **abstract vertical texture** | Full frontal blue-sky postcard tower |
| Side/oblique, partial, fogged, or roof-cropped | Tourist with phone, flags, coach buses |
| Integrate into house + sky stack like a neighbor | Isolate on white or pure black void |
| Suggest scale via small house blocks, not captions | Golden-circle “Iceland in one shot” montage |

## Spring

**Narrative (season of thaw, not Easter product):** Melt, pale sun, first coastal greens, soft wind.  
Aligns with Bright Agency season-of-nature framing (spring themes without bunny/Easter packaging).

| Spec | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **Sky** | Milk-blue to pale grey; thin high cloud; cool-warm mix |
| **Light** | Low, clear, slightly cool; long soft shadows on wool ground |
| **Ground / sea** | Patchy snow melt + spongy green moss; puddle reflections as soft gouache ovals |
| **Houses** | Dusty coral/ochre façades with wet-roof sheen (painted, not photo) |
| **Hallgrímskirkja** | Pale stone-lavender silhouette; basalt fluting readable; base soft-fog |
| **Seasonal motif (subtle)** | Tiny felt crocus or cotton-grass tufts in foreground shelf only - **no eggs, no bunnies, no “spring sale” props** |
| **Mood** | Hopeful, clean, waking - bedtime warmth in cream wool still present |
| **Palette keys** | `#E8F0F4` sky, `#C5D9C3` moss, `#EDE4D8` wool, `#D4A59A` house blush, `#9AABB8` church cool |
| **Forbidden** | Cherry-blossom Japan cues; neon grass; Easter candy colors |

**Composite note:** Mascot sits on dry wool patch in fore-left-of-center safe zone; wordmark above or beside, high contrast on soft sky panel.

**Narrative:** Calm blue Reykjavík winter - short day, soft snow that comes and goes, blue hour. **Not Christmas.**

| Spec | Direction |

| **Mood** | Safe, quiet, plush-warm against cool city - bedtime under winter sky |

## Mascot invariants

**Rule:** Mascot and wordmark are **exact post-composite layers** from official assets. Never AI-recreate, re-illustrate, recolor, or “style-match” them into the background.

### Official assets (authoritative)

| Asset | Path (read-only) | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark | `/home/pk/git/yarnkin/web/src/web/assets/brand/yarnkin-wordmark.png` | Plush lettering - identity |
| Mascot | `/home/pk/git/yarnkin/native/android/design-drops/launcher/yarnball_launcher_1024.png` | Yarnball character - identity |

### Invariants for compositing (do not violate)

| ID | Invariant |
| --- | --- |
| **M1** | Use the official PNG pixel data only; no redraw, no AI “same character” generation |
| **M2** | No recolor, hue-shift, or seasonal wardrobe drawn **onto** the mascot file |
| **M3** | No added props on the mascot (hats, scarves, gifts, glasses) unless a **separate official prop asset** exists (none in brief → none) |
| **M4** | Preserve alpha; soft drop-shadow under mascot may be added at composite stage only if it matches brand shadow language - keep subtle, cool/neutral, not red-green |
| **M5** | Scale consistently across seasons (same relative size to cover height, e.g. mascot ~28–36% of cover height) so family system reads as one system |
| **M6** | Placement: same compositional anchor each season (recommended: **center-right of safe zone** or **center** so mobile crop never clips face/core of yarnball) |
| **M7** | Wordmark: same file; no alternate fonts; no outline bake into background; no seasonal lettering |
| **M8** | Wordmark placement: **upper center or upper center-right of safe zone**; clearance from profile-photo overlap (bottom-left) |
| **M9** | Z-order: background art (text-free) → optional soft ground contact shadow → mascot → wordmark |
| **M10** | Do not mirror, stretch, or heavily filter the mascot; mild global grade on **background only** |
| **M11** | Clearance: ≥24 px (at 851-wide) between wordmark and mascot; no overlapping letterforms on yarn fibers |
| **M12** | Recognition test: at 50% mobile size, yarnball silhouette and wordmark must still read as Yarnkin without season-specific props |

### Background-mascot relationship

- Background may include **yarn texture in the environment** (felt ground, wool haze) that **rhymes** with the mascot material without cloning its face/form.  
- Background characters: optional tiny distant soft shapes only if they cannot be confused with the yarnball mascot. Prefer **no secondary character**.

## Typography recommendations

**Background layer:** **no type** (downstream generates text-free art).

**If any type is later added in a non-cover surface (ads, posts):**

| Use | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary identity** | Official plush wordmark only - never rebuild “Yarnkin” in a free font |
| **Supporting UI type** | Soft rounded geometric sans (system-adjacent: something in the spirit of Nunito / Rounded Mplus / brand’s existing UI if defined) - **verify against live Yarnkin UI before final** |
| **Seasonal headlines** | Short season nouns only if needed elsewhere: “Spring in Reykjavík” style - calm, not festive script |
| **Avoid** | Blackletter, Christmas script, decorative snow fonts, comic bold shout, all-caps condensed sale type on cover |

**Cover itself:** Prefer **zero** extra type beyond wordmark so mobile safe-zone stays clean (matches 2026 cover best practice of one message).
