Sporbók · Áhöfnin · first five shorts

Serious company, deadpan cast

Five silent shorts, 10 to 12 seconds each, rendered frame by frame from the same rig as the brand kit, so every character is exactly on model. Directed here, with Grok 4.7 as co-director: its independent pitches and ours converged on the same three stories, five of its lines made the final cut, and its review of the first renders caught two timing bugs and a caption that read as worker scoring. Captions carry the meaning with the sound off. Each ends on the icon and the tagline.

The spin: object theatreThe cast is the kit, and the person stays the adult in the room. Each object only moves the way that thing moves: a lid, a headlight, a paper edge. Luxo Jr. is the grammar; Michelin's Bibendum proves a serious trade company can keep a character for a century.
The joke is a day you already had07:40 in the dark, a receipt under the seat, Sunday night at the kitchen table. The product is the punchline, never the pitch, and nobody speaks.
Restraint is the brandNo people drawn, no tourism, no confetti. One caption line per shot, no exclamation marks. One joy peak per film at most.

How they go out

Cadence

  • One short a week, alongside the text posts, so the cast is in about one post in three.
  • Timed to its day: 17:00 on a Friday afternoon, Sunnudagskvöld on Sunday evening, 07:40 on a Monday morning.
  • Reels cut to Reels, the 4:5 cut to the feed. Same film, framed for each.

Through the pipeline

  • Each film goes into Postiz as a draft through the social Flow, like every other post, and waits for approval.
  • Post copy follows the social Skills: Icelandic, no bait, no link in the body, the link in the first comment.
  • New films are rendered from the same timeline file, so the cast can never drift off model.

Not yet

  • Sound. The films are silent-first; a later mix would be foley only: rain, a paper slide, a lid, a relay click.
  • Grok's other ideas are on file for the next batch: rain on the open site, and last one home.
Rendered at 30 fps from the Áhöfnin rig (a port of the Yarnkin mascot rig) with a seeded clock, H.264, 0.3 to 1.1 MB each. Characters are never generated by an image or video model.
