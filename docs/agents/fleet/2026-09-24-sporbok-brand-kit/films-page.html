Sporbók · Áhöfnin · generated with Grok Imagine

Five films, directed here, animated by Grok

Every film starts from keyframes rendered by our own character rig, so the cast is exactly on model. Grok 4.7 animates between them with image-to-video and adds foley. Captions, the clock, the job-card lettering and the end card are drawn by us on top, so no model ever draws a letter. Each cut was inspected frame by frame here and by two independent reviewers, and re-shot until the faces stayed friendly all the way through: 27 generated takes for 8 final shots.

How a film is made

Four steps, each owned by whoever is best at it. A new film is a new timeline and a new shot spec; the pipeline does not change.

Director (Claude)Story and timelineBeats, captions and poses written as a timeline on the rig. Every pose is chosen, never a random pool.
RigKeyframesText-free, blink-free frames at the moments that matter, plus a model sheet per character.
Grok 4.7Image-to-videoreference_to_video with first, key and last frames pinned, the model sheets as references, and a style lock. 720p, 24 fps, foley.
AssemblerText, cards, soundConform to 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, draw captions with ink picked from the picture's measured brightness, mask card lettering behind characters, end card, loudness to −18 LUFS, a 4:5 cut.
QAFrame by frameContact sheets at 4 fps, zoom on every face, scene-jump detection, stream checks, plus an independent reviewer.

What the generator gets wrong, and the fix

  • Half-closed eyes in its in-betweens turn into a V-shaped scowl. Fix: never ask for a half-closed stage; pin open, then closed, 1.3 s apart.
  • It redraws text as gibberish. Fix: every card is blank in the keyframes; we draw the lettering, masked where a character passes in front.
  • It adds shading, grain and moving floor shadows. Fix: a style lock on every prompt and model sheets as references.
  • Overlapping characters can merge into one with extra eyes. Fix: characters never overlap in a keyframe.

Rules the rig now enforces

  • No eye-roll: an open eye never hides its pupil under the lid.
  • No side-eye: a strong sideways look caps the smiling lower lid.
  • No squint: a heavy upper lid never meets a raised lower lid, and heavy lids flatten.
  • A "z" vanishes the instant the eyes open.
