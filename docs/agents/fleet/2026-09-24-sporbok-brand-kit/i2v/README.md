# Áhöfnin films: rig keyframes, Grok image-to-video, our text

Five silent-first shorts (Reels 9:16 and feed 4:5) whose motion is generated by Grok Imagine and whose look, poses, text and sound levels are ours.

## Pipeline

1. Direct: a timeline in `../video.html` (`VIDEOS.*`) with explicit moods at every beat; no random mood pools in films.
2. Keyframes: `node ../keyframes.cjs <film> <outdir> t1 t2 ... [t@kind=mood,...]` renders text-free, blink-free frames (`?clean=1` hides captions and card lettering); `--sheets` renders one model sheet per character.
3. Generate: a shot spec (`specs/`) for Grok 4.7, executed with its `reference_to_video` tool: first, key and last frames pinned, model sheets as `images`, a style lock appended to every prompt, 720p.
   Tool schemas as reported by Grok: `grok-media-tools.md`.
4. Assemble: `node ../assemble.cjs <film> clip1.mp4 [clip2.mp4] [--feed]` conforms to 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, measures picture brightness behind the text (ink is chosen from it, with hysteresis), draws captions, clock, kicker and end card from `../overlay.html`, draws job-card lettering from `../cards.js` masked where a character passes in front, normalises loudness to −18 LUFS and crops the 4:5 cut at a per-film offset.
5. QA: `../qa.sh <video> <outdir>` makes 4 fps contact sheets, a scene-jump list and stream and loudness stats; every film was reviewed frame by frame here and twice by an independent reviewer.

## What the generator gets wrong, and the rule that fixed it

- Half-closed eyes in its in-betweens become a V-shaped scowl: never ask for a half-closed stage; pin open, then fully closed, 1.3 s apart.
- It redraws text as gibberish: keyframes carry blank cards; lettering is ours.
- Overlapping characters can merge into one with extra eyes: never let characters overlap in a keyframe.
- Body cues such as a nod get over-acted into a tilt: a prompt that asks for "a nod" now says the body does not move.
- It invents moving floor shadows: the style lock says the ground is flat, empty and still.
- xAI's image-to-video limit is 2 requests per second: at most 2 calls at a time, retry a 429 after 20 s.

Rig rules added along the way (in `../cast.js`): no eye-roll, no side-eye (a sideways look caps the lower lid), no squint (a heavy upper lid never meets a raised lower lid, and heavy lids flatten), a `drowsy` pose with level lids, no blink in keyframes, and a z that vanishes when the eyes open.

## v2: feature films (Mætt, Nótan, Drögin, Frítíminn)

Four 24.2-second films (20 s of story plus an end card carrying the spoken tagline), each showing real Sporbók features instead of mood alone.

| Film | Feature | Final clips |
|---|---|---|
| maett (Mætt) | arrival recorded at the site, time on the right job, confirm the day | maett3/shot-A, rig 7-14 s (`render-rig.cjs`), maett3/shot-C |
| nota (Nótan) | supplier stop prompt, receipt read and matched to the job | nota3/shot-A, B, C |
| drog (Drögin) | invoice draft (Vinna, Efni og kvittanir, Akstur, totals that add up), sent to Reiknís | drog3/shot-A, B, C |
| fri2 (Frítíminn) | the worker ends the shift, location off, day summary | fri22/hybrid-A (Grok 0-4.4 s, rig after a 0.4 s crossfade), fri23/shot-B, C |

Pipeline changes:

1. Picture: keyframes from `../video.html` as before; generated with `reference_to_video` from the specs in `specs/shots-v2*.md`.
2. Text and app UI are a separate alpha layer, `../ui.html`: clock or kicker, one headline, cards and chips, each in its own zone inside the 4:5 window (y 285-1635 of the 1920 master), headlines limited to the left column so they never cross the sun or the moon.
3. `node ../assemble2.cjs <film> clipA clipB clipC [--feed]` composites the layer, places UI sounds from `../sfx/` at the event times the layer reports, adds `vo/<film>/lines.json` voiceover and a music bed if present, and masters in two passes to -14 LUFS under a -1.5 dBTP limiter.
4. `node ../render-rig.cjs <film> t0 t1 out.mp4` renders a stretch straight from the rig, as the fallback when a generated stretch cannot be made clean.
5. Voiceover: `vo/script.json` plus `node ../vo.mjs <film|all>` (Gemini TTS, voice Alnilam, takes checked by transcribing them back); needs a paid Gemini key, since the free tier allows 10 TTS requests a day and is not cleared for commercial use in the EEA.
6. Music: generated by Grok as the soundtrack of a still, dark 15 s clip (`specs/music-spec.md`), picked by a Gemini listening pass, looped to 23.5 s with an equal-power crossfade; the picture is discarded.

What v2 added to the list of generator failures:

- Sound design text leaks into the picture: "a trolley rolling past" produced a trolley. Every sound is now described as off screen, and the lock says nothing appears or disappears beyond what the prompt describes.
- A prompt that asks for a lid to close or for a hop is taken literally: the toolbox shut its face away, the receipt jumped and a door appeared under it.
- It anticipates the next shot: asked for a sunset at the job house, it grew the evening home behind it early, three takes in a row. The fix was a crossfade to the rig for that stretch.
- One call returned an all-black video with no error: every call is now checked for mean luma above 40.
- Its own audio stayed at -40 to -60 LUFS even when asked for clear sound design; boosting it made hiss, gating it per shot made a 5-6 dB drop at each join, and its hard cuts clicked. The mix no longer uses clip audio.
- `image_to_video` accepts only 6 or 10 s; `reference_to_video` accepts up to 15 s.
- Raised lower lids (a smile) read as ghost eyes on the receipt and as a smug squint on the toolbox, and generated in-betweens turn them into half-lids: films use the open-eyed `glad` expression for those two.
- A ramped rig blink at 30 fps lands on half-shut frames that read as a glare; the rig blink is now binary (shut for 0.1 s).
- The van's eyes narrowed into a glare in four generated takes of Mætt B; that stretch is rendered by the rig, which cuts in seamlessly because the generated shots are pinned to rig keyframes at both ends.
- An independent review caught numbers that did not add up (hours against clock times, an invoice total against its lines); every film's UI numbers are now derived from one story.

Review page: https://claude.ai/artifact/ADWY28CEabmBwkuHiMh9bS (private until shared); `../films-v2-page.html` is its source.

Sound, final:

- Music: one Lyria cue per film on the paid AI Studio project (`lyria.mjs`, prompts in `music/prompts.json`). Lyria ignores requested lengths and endings (clips run 30 s, Lyria 3.5 ran 46 and 128 s), so each chosen take is cut on its bar grid so its own final cadence lands at 20.25 s (`music/edit.json`: take, cadence, trim, tempo within 6%). Takes were picked in two blind Gemini listening passes with the order reversed, because the first pass put take A first in 3 of 4 films.
- Voice: `vo.mjs` with Gemini TTS voice Alnilam; every take is transcribed back and must match the script. The tagline is one chosen take shared by all films (`vo/script.json` tagline.file): 5 of 6 takes of "Sporbók man söguna" were heard as "mannsögunnar".
- Master: two-pass, -14 LUFS, limiter at -2.0 dB so the AAC encode stays under -1.5 dBTP.
