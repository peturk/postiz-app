Four films that show what Sporbók does

Each film follows one real part of the working day: arriving at the job, the stop at the supplier, the invoice draft, and ending the shift. Grok 4.7 animates the cast from keyframes rendered by our rig. The app screens, text and sounds are ours, drawn on top, so every label is real Sporbók wording. The whole story is told in 20 seconds, then the end card carries the spoken tagline.

The films

Sound on: an Icelandic voiceover, an original score for each film, and a tap, pop or chime on every app action.

Format

Mætt 07:48 → 07:52

The van reaches the job at dawn; Sporbók records the arrival by itself.

  • Geofence ring, "Mætt 07:52", "Á vakt"
  • Time counts up to 4 klst. 10 mín. on the right job
  • You confirm the day with one tap

Nótan 12:10

A stop at the builders' merchant: one tap, and the receipt is read and put on the right job.

  • "Þú komst við hjá birgja. Hvert var erindið?"
  • Receipt read: BYKO, 14.820 kr.
  • "Passar við verk", nothing left unbilled

Drögin verklok

The job is done and the invoice draft builds itself: labour, materials and receipts, mileage.

  • Lines fill in and add up: 69.678 kr. before VAT
  • "Senda í Reiknís", then "Reikningur sendur"
  • Only live integrations are shown

Frítíminn 17:30

You end the shift, location switches off, and the evening belongs to you.

  • "Ljúka vakt", then "Utan vaktar"
  • Sunset, then a moon that stays in view
  • Summary 9 klst. 38 mín. (07:52 to 17:30)

What changed since the last round

Fixed

They show the product

  • Real Sporbók screens in every film: clock, geofence, notification, receipt scan, invoice draft, shift end
  • Something new happens every 1.5 to 3 seconds
  • Text sits in fixed zones, so nothing collides, and it stays inside the 4:5 crop
Fixed

Sun and moon

  • The moon is now a lit crescent with a glow, so it stays visible against the night sky
  • The sun sets behind the ground instead of fading out
  • Headlines use the left column only, so they never cross the sun or moon
Fixed

Sound

  • A scored cue per film and a real voiceover (see Sound below)
  • Every tap, pop, notification and chime lands on its app action
  • No level dips or clicks at the shot joins
Fixed

After an independent review

  • Numbers agree everywhere: hours, the invoice total and the day summary add up
  • Cards sit 90 px in from the edge and never cover the sun
  • Faces stay open-eyed and friendly: no grey eyes, glares or lids that shut over a face
  • Frítíminn cuts straight to the lit home instead of a double-exposure dissolve
  • Less dead time: something new happens every 1.5 to 3 seconds
Checked

Frame by frame

  • 40 generated picture takes for 12 final shots
  • Takes rejected for invented objects: a trolley, a door, scaffolding, a floating house, a second house
  • Also rejected for an all-black clip and a character waking up again
  • Two stretches Grok could not keep clean (Mætt 7-14 s, Frítíminn 4-7 s) are rendered by our own rig, cut in on the same pinned frames

Sound

Scored

Music: one cue per film

  • Composed with Google Lyria on the paid AI Studio project: one shared palette (fingerpicked guitar, felt piano, marimba, round bass, brushes) with a rising three-note glockenspiel signature
  • Mætt: dawn into a working groove. Nótan: light and bouncy. Drögin: builds as the numbers add up, then settles. Frítíminn: golden hour into a lullaby
  • 3 to 5 takes per film, picked in two blind listening passes with the order reversed to cancel position bias
  • Each cue is edited on its bar lines so its own final cadence lands as the end card appears
Recorded

Voice: Alnilam, Gemini TTS

  • Every take is transcribed back and must match the script word for word; up to 6 takes on the hard lines
  • "Sporbók man söguna" was heard as "mannsögunnar" in 5 of 6 takes; the one clean take is shared by all four films
  • The music ducks under the voice and comes back up between lines
  • Mastered to -14 LUFS, true peak under -1.4 dBTP
