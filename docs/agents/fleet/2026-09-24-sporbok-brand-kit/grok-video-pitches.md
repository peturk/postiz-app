# Sporbók: eight short films

For the lead. Creative only.

## The worry

The spin is object theatre: the cast is the kit, and the person holding it stays the adult in the room.
Each character is a thing already on the job, and it only moves the way that thing moves: a lid, a headlight, a torn paper edge, a glance in the glass.
Pixar's Luxo Jr. (1986) is the grammar: a lamp with eyes, no dialogue, feeling carried by the hinge and the timing, the object still a lamp at the end.
Michelin's Bibendum is the trade proof: a serious tire company has used a character with garages for a century, at human scale, in work colours, with no children's-show energy.
Leave Duolingo's Duo and the louder Mailchimp Freddie years alone; those characters want something from the viewer, and that want is what makes a work tool look like a kids' app.
Sporbók's face stays small, the timing stays slow, the palette stays rain blue, graphite and hi-vis, and the joke is a Tuesday the viewer has already had.
A frame is right when it would still read with the eyes removed; the eyes only tell you which object is paying attention.
These eight use nod, hum, wiggle, jolt, the oops drop, the lid snap and asleep. Joy, hop and sparkles wait.

## How to shoot all eight

One cut serves both 1080x1920 and 1080x1350.
Action and the caption sit in the center 1080 by 1350. The extra reel height is only floor, rain or a low grey sky.
Captions are work words: kvittun, verk, vakt, reikningur. Cast names stay in the shot list.
One caption line per shot. A shot may carry none.
Ship silent. If a mix is added later, the only audio is foley: rain, a paper slide, a lid, a headlight relay.
No people, no hands, no aurora, no puffins, no waterfalls, no Ring Road, no mountain hero shot, no confetti.
The product name appears on the last frame only.

Same last frame on every film, about three seconds.
Light grey brand field, `sporbok-icon.png`, live text in the brand font on two lines: "Þú vinnur verkið." and "Sporbók man söguna."
The characters are already gone.

## 1. Undir sætinu

**Beat:** Stuck under the seat -> slides out onto the right job -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 12s.
**Cast:** Nótan anxious with a small wiggle, then relieved. Bíllinn fond, one nod, headlights off.

- **0-4s.** Tight under the seat, dark flat grey, a slice of rain-blue door above. Nótan is wedged in, anxious, torn tail in a small wiggle. Caption: "Undir sætinu aftur."
- **4-9s.** She slides onto the concrete beside one plain job card, relieved. Bíllinn's windscreen eyes look down, fond, one nod. Caption: "Á réttu verki."
- **9-12s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** Every driver has a receipt graveyard under the seat, and the bookkeeper is who they send this to.
The laugh is that it happened again, and the warmth is that it landed right.

## 2. 07:40

**Beat:** Empty dark site -> the van is already there, unbothered -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 12s.
**Cast:** Bíllinn busy as he settles, then content, a slow hum. Headlights on.

- **0-4s.** Dark morning, rain, a flat grey road, empty concrete. Caption: "07:40."
- **4-9s.** Bíllinn is already parked, rain blue, content, a slow hum. Caption: "Fyrstur á verkstað."
- **9-12s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** The person who is always first drops this in the crew chat and writes nothing under it.
He looks competent, and the film stays quiet about it.

## 3. Lokið

**Beat:** The box is still checking -> the lid snaps shut and the hat sleeps -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 13s.
**Cast:** Kassinn busy in a narrow peek, then asleep on the snap. Hjálmurinn neutral, eyes on the floor, then closed. He never looks around the site.

- **0-4s.** Dusk, concrete, low grey sky. Kassinn's lid is a narrow peek, busy. Hjálmurinn rests beside him, steady, eyes on the floor. Caption: "Eitt enn."
- **4-9s.** The lid snaps shut. Kassinn is asleep. Hjálmurinn's eyes close downward. Caption: "Slokknar eftir vakt."
- **9-13s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** Crews pass this on because the kit goes dark when the day ends.
The snap is the privacy promise in one move.

## 4. Sunnudagskvöld

**Beat:** One receipt awake on a Sunday table -> she settles, and the invoice is simply ready -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 13s.
**Cast:** Nótan thinking and anxious, then relieved, then content. No hop.

- **0-4s.** A kitchen table, one warm lamp, Sunday night, flat vector. Nótan is alone, thinking, torn tail still. Caption: "Sunnudagskvöld."
- **4-9s.** She settles flat, relieved, then content. The table stays bare around her. Caption: "Reikningurinn er tilbúinn."
- **9-13s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** This is the bookkeeper's film.
The person who does the books on Sunday sends it to the owner, because the evening can go back to being an evening.

## 5. Tvö verk

**Beat:** One receipt between two jobs -> she lands on one of them -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 12s.
**Cast:** Nótan thinking, a small jolt, then relieved, one nod.

- **0-4s.** An empty supplier counter, two plain job cards, no clerk. Nótan stands upright between them, thinking. Caption: "Tvö verk. Ein kvittun."
- **4-9s.** A small jolt, and she lands on one card, relieved, one nod. The other card stays where it was. Caption: "Þetta verkið."
- **9-12s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** A receipt on the wrong job is the mistake that costs real money.
Anyone running two sites in a day sends this to the person who does the costing.

## 6. Rigningin

**Beat:** Rain on the open site -> the lid holds -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 12s.
**Cast:** Kassinn content in a thin peek, one oops jolt, then content again. Hjálmurinn neutral and unmoved, rain on the shell.

- **0-3s.** Rain on concrete, horizon as a flat grey band. Caption: "Rignir."
- **3-8s.** Kassinn holds a thin peek. A gust, one oops jolt and a single sweat drop, then the lid settles, content. Hjálmurinn does not move. Caption: "Ekkert komst inn."
- **8-12s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** Weather is the job.
Carpenters and electricians send this because a box that stays shut is competence they already recognise.

## 7. Síðastur heim

**Beat:** The van is last on the road -> the headlights go out -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 14s.
**Cast:** Bíllinn content with headlights on, then headlights out, asleep.

- **0-5s.** Dusk, a flat grey road, horizon low, no mountains. Bíllinn rolls, content, headlights on. Caption: "Síðastur heim."
- **5-10s.** Parked. The headlights go out. The eyes close. Asleep. Caption: "Svo slokknar."
- **10-14s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** The owner who leaves last keeps this one.
It carries the same off-switch as Lokið, at the speed of a drive home.

## 8. Á staðnum

**Beat:** The van stops on the concrete -> a time sits on screen -> icon and tagline.
**Length:** 13s.
**Cast:** Bíllinn busy, then content, one nod. Hjálmurinn already on the concrete, eyes on the floor, then a small nod. No hands.

- **0-4s.** Rain easing, concrete site. Bíllinn rolls in and stops. Hjálmurinn is already resting on the concrete, eyes on the floor. No caption.
- **4-9s.** Hold. Bíllinn gives one nod. Caption: "Á staðnum 07:52."
- **9-13s.** End card.

**Why it gets sent:** Arrival time is the quiet argument on a job.
The office sends this because the time is simply there, and nobody is accused.

## Top 3

1. **Undir sætinu.** Highest recognition, and it teaches the cast without introducing them. Driver and bookkeeper both have someone to send it to.
2. **Sunnudagskvöld.** The money emotion, on the night the books actually happen. The person who pays the bills is the sharer.
3. **Lokið.** The lid snap is the whole company in one gesture, and it is the frame that keeps the cartoon inside a work rule.

Síðastur heim is the close call: same off-switch, more mood, fewer people it gets forwarded to.
Shoot 1, 4 and 3 first.
