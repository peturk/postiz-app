3. My recommendation

Calm is the brand voice; whisper is an effect. Brand voice rules say quiet and parent to parent, not ASMR: a whisper is intimate for a few, off-putting for others, and less intelligible on a phone speaker. The tagline is the one sound heard in every film and every time of day, so it should be calm and full (Bedtime reader, calm direction, keeps the voice the series already uses). Whisper, if at all, only inside the Góða nótt story after the lamp goes out.
