# Scoring rubric: Yarnkin film ads (100 points)

Built on Google's ABCDs of effective video ads (Attention, Branding, Connection, Direction; 17,000+ campaigns with Ipsos, reviewed by Nielsen and Kantar), Meta's 2026 Reels practice (hook in the first 3 s, design for sound-off, safe zones: top 14% and bottom 35% of a 9:16 frame are covered by UI), and Ehrenberg-Bass distinctive brand assets (consistent assets build memory).

| Dimension | Points | What earns the points |
|---|---|---|
| A. Attention | 20 | The first 3 s make a parent stop: movement, a question or a feeling, not an empty set. Something new every 2-3 s; no dead stretches. |
| B. Branding | 20 | The brand is present early without front-loading a logo; distinctive assets (the four mascots, the palette, the music-box signature, one consistent tagline voice) recur; the brand is identifiable with sound off. |
| C. Connection | 25 | The film speaks to a specific parent and a real moment; emotion is earned, not stated; the voice casting fits the film's message and audience; the Icelandic sounds native and warm. |
| D. Direction | 15 | The viewer knows what to do next and why, without a hard sell (brand rules forbid pressure and an app-download call to action in the hook): a true, low-friction next step. |
| E. Craft and compliance | 20 | Sound-off legible (text size, contrast, reading time); critical content inside the Reels safe zones; claims true to the product; no immersion breaks; audio mix clean. |

Score each film per dimension, give the total, name the single weakest point and the one change that would raise the score most.
