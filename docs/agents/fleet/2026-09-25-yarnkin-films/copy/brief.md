# Copy brief: four Yarnkin films (Icelandic)

## Product (facts; nothing beyond these may be claimed)
Yarnkin is a picture-book library for families in Iceland: picture books to read together in the evening, in Icelandic and English.
Three books are open straight away, no account or login needed ("Byrjaðu strax í kvöld, án innskráningar").
Books are for ages 0-10. No ads. No endless feed. Nothing hurries you along ("Enginn rekur á eftir þér").
The reader has a one-tap language switch between Icelandic and English on the same page.
Membership (Lesandi) opens every book on the shelf; new stories every week; every book is reviewed by people.
Official Icelandic tagline (end card, fixed): "Lesum saman. Hlustum saman. Dreymum saman."
Hero line on the site: "Saga fyrir kvöldið."

## Never claim or imply
Offline reading; "unlimited", "forever", "most popular", "exceptional quality"; that pictures are hand drawn; read-aloud or narration (the books shown do not have it); sleep guarantees ("your child will fall asleep"); developmental or learning claims; parental guilt ("you never have time"); pressure; an app-download call to action.

## Audience and voice
Parents of young children in Iceland. Parent to parent, like a calm, capable local adult. Warm, bedtime, storybook, Reykjavík. Quiet, never a toy commercial, never a translated slogan. Natural, correctly edited Icelandic, written in Icelandic from the start. Never use the em dash; use a comma, a period or a plain hyphen.

## Format
Each film is 20 s of silent story (no voice) with one short headline on screen at a time, then the end card.
A headline must be readable in its slot: at most about 5 words, one line where possible, max 2 short lines.
The picture shows the mascots (Bangsi the bear, Refur the fox, Ugla the owl, Kanína the rabbit) and a real picture book on screen ("Óskar telur ræturnar", a counting book: "Óskar telur. Einar rótardyr.").

## The films (new staging)
1. kvold (0-20 s): early evening. Bangsi and Kanína on the sofa, the window turns from dusk to night, a lamp comes on. The three open book covers appear, one is tapped, the book opens on page 1 and page 2. Beats: 0.5-3.5, 3.6-7.6, 7.8-12.0, 12.2-16.0, 16.2-19.9.
2. saman (0-20 s): reading together. Bangsi in an armchair reads the book; Ugla and Kanína on floor cushions listen; pages turn 1 to 4. Beats: 0.4-4.0, 4.0-8.0, 8.0-12.0, 12.0-16.0, 16.0-19.9.
3. tunga (0-20 s): the same page switches from Icelandic ("Óskar telur. Bank, bank, bank.") to English ("Oskar counts. Tap, tap, tap.") with one tap, then back; Refur and Kanína on a window seat. Beats: 0.4-5.8, 6.0-12.2, 12.4-15.4, 15.6-19.9.
4. nott (0-20 s): bedtime. Everyone in bed under a blanket, Bangsi reads the last page ("Óskar telur. Góða nótt, bjartar stjörnur."), lowers the book, the lamp goes out, one by one they fall asleep; moon in the window. Beats: 0.4-4.4, 4.6-9.0, 9.2-14.2, 14.4-19.9.
