Yarnkin · four films · the real cast and real books

Bedtime films, now with a voice

Four short films in the same spirit as the Sporbók ones, each showing one true part of reading with Yarnkin: an open book tonight, reading together, two languages, and good night. The mascots are rendered frame by frame by Yarnkin's own character component, the pages are real catalog pages of Óskar telur ræturnar, and every word on a screen is the app's own Icelandic.

The films

Sound on. Each film has its own voice from WTD's Voice Bank, cast for what it says: Grandmother storyteller (Saga fyrir kvöldið), Father (Lesið saman), Mother (Á íslensku og ensku), Bedtime reader (Góða nótt). The tagline over every end card is always the Bedtime reader: one consistent sound for the brand.

Format

Saga fyrir kvöldið

Dusk on the sofa; the lamp comes on; three open books; one opens.

  • Rökkrið læðist inn.
  • Kveikjum á litla lampanum.
  • Þrjár bækur standa opnar.
  • Engin innskráning, sagan hefst.
  • Saga fyrir kvöldið.

Lesið saman

Bangsi reads in the armchair; Ugla and Kanína listen from their cushions.

  • Einn les, aðrir hlusta.
  • Enginn rekur á eftir okkur.
  • Skoðum myndirnar í rólegheitum.
  • Blaðsíðu fyrir blaðsíðu.
  • Tíminn fær að bíða.

Á íslensku og ensku

On the daybed, one tap turns the page from Icelandic to English and back.

  • Sagan hefst á íslensku.
  • Einn smellur, orðin verða ensk.
  • Ljúft að flakka á milli.
  • Sama sagan, tvö mál.

Góða nótt

Everyone in bed: the last page, the lamp goes out, one by one they sleep.

  • Allir komnir undir hlýja sæng.
  • „Góða nótt, bjartar stjörnur.“
  • Lampinn slokknar, kyrrðin tekur við.
  • Draumarnir geta byrjað.

How they were made

Brand rule

Nothing generated in the picture

  • Yarnkin's brand rules forbid generating or redrawing the mascots or story art, so there is no image-to-video step
  • The cast is Yarnkin's own <Mascot> component, unchanged, played frame by frame under a controlled clock
  • The book art is the real catalog: the three open books and their pages
Round 3

What changed

  • No popups or tap circles: the book itself carries the story
  • The cast sits on a sofa, an armchair, cushions and in bed under a blanket instead of standing in a row
  • The lamp moved off the reader; the owl sits on the bedhead
  • Every headline was scored and rewritten by a Gemini 3.8 Flash critic acting as a native Icelandic editor (via the agy client)
Checked

Faces, words, layout

  • Faces cast from a grid of real rig poses; no eye-rolls, side-eye or squints (an independent review caught the first ones)
  • Every on-screen string is from the app; the no-login line is its pricing copy, verified against the open books
  • Everything sits inside the 4:5 crop; cards clear every face
Scored

A lullaby for each film

  • Lyria, one palette: felt piano, celesta, nylon guitar, pizzicato, clarinet, music box, and a falling four-note signature
  • Three takes per film, picked in two blind listening passes with the order reversed
  • Each cue cut on its own bar lines so its final chord lands on the end card; mastered to -14 LUFS
