Both scorers agree: Attention and Direction are the weak dimensions; Connection and Branding are strong.
v5 changes only what the scores point at, keeps every string, voice take and asset from v4 unless a change below names it, and is scored the same way against v4.

## 1. Attention: the product and motion in the first second (target +4 to +6 on A)

Meta 2026: the first 3 s decide the hook rate; v4 opened every film on a still room and showed the product late (kvold 7.8 s).

- kvold: the three open covers are on screen from 0.3 s over the dusk; the lamp comes on at 3.5 s; at 7.8 s the covers light up one by one under "Þrjár bækur standa opnar."; the first cover is tapped at 11 s and the reader opens at 12.2 s.
- saman: the reader is open from 0.3 s and the first page turns at 2.0 s, so the film starts mid-read.
- tunga: the first language switch moves from 6.0 s to 2.8 s; the headlines and voice lines move with it (same takes, re-timed).
- nott: the book is on screen from 0.3 s, as before.
- Every first headline appears at 0.2 s.

v4 pointed to Yarnkin only on the end card.

- saman and nott: a quiet chip "3 bækur opnar · yarnkin.com" during the second half of the story (true: three books open without an account).
- End card: URL 40 → 56 px, the next-step line 38 → 44 px.
