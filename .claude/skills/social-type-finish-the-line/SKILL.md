# Finish-the-line type

Shape only.
Brand, move, and channel rules live in other Skills.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Slots

Fill:

- `HOOK`: an incomplete sentence ending in an ellipsis
- `FLAVOR`: optional first comment - the brand's own completion

### Step 2: Post body

One sentence, cut where completion is fun.
The unfinished part must have many good answers, not one right answer.
The invitation is implicit in the ellipsis; do not add "finish this sentence" or any completion instruction - that is engagement bait (see `social-move-engage` Step 2).

### Step 3: First comment

Optional.
The brand completes the line its own way, once, to set the tone.
Never an instruction.

## Examples

`HOOK`: `Besta kvöldið er þegar sagan endar og ...`
`FLAVOR`: `... Bangsi er löngu sofnaður.`

## Troubleshooting

Issue: everyone writes the same completion
Cause: the sentence has one obvious ending
Solution: cut earlier, where imagination takes over

Issue: the hook says "kláraðu setninguna"
Cause: explicit completion instruction
Solution: delete it; the ellipsis is the whole invitation
