# Moment type

Shape only.
Brand, move, and channel rules live in other Skills.
This is the no-media sibling of `still`: when there is a real photo, use `still`; when the moment is told in words on a color card, use this.

## Instructions

### Step 1: Slots

Fill:

- `BEAT`: one or two short sentences, one concrete moment
- `PAYOFF`: optional first comment - the closing line, or a link

### Step 2: Post body

At most two sentences.
Concrete nouns, present tense, no question mark.
The moment must stand alone for a reader who never comments.

### Step 3: First comment

Optional.
The payoff or a link, never a question.

## Examples

`BEAT`: `Í kvöld leitar Ugla að tunglinu. Það hefur falið sig bak við ský.`
No comment.

## Troubleshooting

Issue: it became three sentences
Cause: explaining the moment
Solution: keep the moment, delete the explanation

Issue: a question crept in
Cause: engage habit
Solution: this shape never asks; move the question to an engage Ticket
