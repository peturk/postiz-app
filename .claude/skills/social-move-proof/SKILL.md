# Proof move

Goal: trust. The reader gets something; the Page asks for nothing.
Brand voice and channel limits live in other Skills.

Battle-test origin, 2026-08-31: a question-only week is empty question-asking - a follower who does not feel like commenting gets nothing, and net follows die (peer critique in `docs/agents/fleet/2026-08-31-compose-battle-test/`).

## Instructions

### Step 1: Give, do not ask

The post is a gift: a story detail, a stealable ritual, a field tip, a job that went right.
No question. No CTA. No "what about you?".

### Step 2: Show, do not claim

One concrete moment beats any adjective.
A wet dashboard beats "reliable in all weather".
Ugla looking for the moon behind a cloud beats "magical stories".
Never fabricate a customer, a job, or a testimonial.

### Step 3: First comment

Optional: the payoff line, or a link (links live in the first comment, never the body - see `social-channel-facebook`).
Never a question stacked on the gift.

### Step 4: Success

Success is saves, shares, and quiet return visits, not comments.
Harvest judges this move by reach and follows, not reply counts.

## Examples

Topic: rain and the paper-vs-tablet reality.
Post: `Þegar rignir fer spjaldtölvan í poka en snertiskjárinn virkar áfram. Blaðið á mælaborðinu blotnar fyrst.`
No comment needed.

## Troubleshooting

Issue: the post ends with a question anyway
Cause: engage habit
Solution: delete the question; the gift stands alone

Issue: it reads like an ad
Cause: claiming instead of showing
Solution: cut adjectives, keep the one concrete moment
